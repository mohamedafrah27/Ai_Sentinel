# Social Engineering Tactics and Psychological Exploitation

## Core Principles of Social Engineering in Cyber Attacks
Social engineering exploits cognitive biases and psychological triggers to convince users to circumvent rational security procedures and surrender sensitive credentials or funds.

## Prevalent Psychological Triggers
1. Artificial Urgency: "Your account will be suspended in 24 hours unless verified immediately." This induces fear of loss and forces hasty decisions.
2. Authority Impersonation: Pretending to be IT support, law enforcement, executive management, or tax agencies to compel obedience.
3. Fear and Intimidation: Fabricating notices of unauthorized access, security breaches, or legal liability to cause alarm.
4. Greed and Incentive (Baiting): Offering free crypto distributions, lottery prizes, counterfeit gift cards, or unrealistic investment returns to entice victims to link digital wallets or share credit card numbers.
5. Scarcity and Exclusivity: Simulating limited-time registration windows for high-demand services.

## Defense Strategies
Maintain heightened skepticism whenever a message combines urgent demands with links requesting sensitive credentials. Always pause and independently verify communications through out-of-band contact channels.
