# Phishing Attacks and Deceptive Campaigns

## Definition and Overview
Phishing is a form of social engineering where attackers deceive users into revealing confidential information such as login credentials, financial accounts, credit card numbers, and session cookies. Phishing attacks mimic legitimate entities including major banks, technology providers, streaming services, and government organizations.

## Common Phishing Delivery Vectors
1. Email Phishing: Mass distribution of deceptive emails containing spoofed links or urgency-laden security notices.
2. Spear Phishing: Highly targeted attacks tailored to specific individuals or organizations utilizing reconnaissance data.
3. Smishing and Vishing: Phishing conducted via SMS text messages or automated voice communications.
4. Clone Phishing: Replicating a legitimate, previously delivered email and replacing genuine links with malicious alternatives.

## Phishing Infrastructure and Operation
Attackers typically host phishing sites on compromised web servers, free hosting providers, or bulletproof hosting providers using deceptive domain registrations. Once a victim visits the phishing website and enters credentials, the site captures the input in real-time, stores it in an attacker-controlled database or forwards it via automated webhooks, and then redirects the victim to the authentic service to prevent immediate suspicion.
